fix(a14): host-tool libbpf compat with glibc >= 2.38 runners

The 6.1.68 copy of tools/lib/bpf fails to build with -Werror on
ubuntu-24.04 runners because glibc string.h is const-correct for
strchr() and clang 17 promotes the discard-qualifiers warning:

  libbpf.c:10740:14: error: assigning to 'char *' from 'const char *'

Fix (host helper only, the kernel Image is untouched):
- cast the strchr() result at tools/lib/bpf/libbpf.c
- drop -Werror from tools/lib/bpf/Makefile as a safety net

Triggered via tag v1.1.1.
